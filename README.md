# One-Line Office Jokes

A single original office joke for the make.fun bounty "One-Line Office Jokes". The line turns on a painfully familiar office detail — the shared printer that only ever jams at the worst possible moment — and lands the laugh in the last few words, when the colleague who said "just print it" turns out to be the least helpful person in the room: *"The office printer only jams when the boss is standing behind you, the deadline is in five minutes, and someone has already said 'just print it — it's easy'."*
